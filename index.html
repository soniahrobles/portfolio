About Me

I am a student at the University of California, Santa Barbara, pursuing a major in Communication with minors in Applied Psychology and Media Arts & Design. My academic journey connects creative expression with human understanding, giving me a unique perspective on how media, design, and storytelling shape experience.

With skills in Web Narratives, Digital Fusion, Laser Cutting and design tools such as Adobe Creative Suite, Canva, and Microsoft applications (certified), I enjoy exploring both traditional and emerging technologies. This portfolio reflects my passion for experimenting across mediums; blending art, communication, and design to create projects that are both visually engaging and conceptually meaningful.

Here, you’ll find a selection of my work that highlights this intersection of creativity and innovation.

Louis Vuitton Case Project

Strategic case study on balancing heritage, sustainability, and next-gen luxury. Click the preview to open the full slides (with fullscreen) on the project page.

Layered Realities

A light-reactive, three-layered painting where RGB illumination reveals different narratives with 3D-printed flora and laser-cut mechanics.

Laser Cut Hands

A contemporary take on woodworking through digital fabrication; designed and laser-cut hands balancing tradition and technology.

Playing Cards Wooden Box

Fusion-modeled, laser-cut box using press-fit locks with a custom sun window that reveals the deck and adds a distinct design detail.

Jeff Koons Inspired Collage

Bold layering of everyday symbols—lips, animals, florals, and architecture—turning the ordinary into spectacle.

Postcard Collage

A familiar destination reimagined as a dreamscape blending color, memory, and the pull of future travel.

UCSB Collage

A vibrant tribute to UCSB’s coastal spirit—student life, landmarks, and bright color bursts.

Fresas Collage

Girlhood through brands, trends, and shared rituals. Strawberries nod to the Mexican slang “fresa,” describing a girly-girl.
